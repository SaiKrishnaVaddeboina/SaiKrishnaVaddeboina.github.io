01 · Featured Capstone · Industry-Sponsored ML

Detecting Fare Evasion on MBTA Commuter Rail

A Hidden Markov Model + Multi-Signal Anomaly Scoring framework — sponsored by Masabi × Gemsen.

Built a four-layer machine-learning pipeline that surfaces 15,195 fare-evading riders that the existing rule-based fraud system completely misses — a 20.5% novelty rate that exceeded our industry partner's success threshold. Processed 2.9M activation–scan events across 221K riders on 13 commuter rail lines spanning April 2017–January 2026. Engineered 86 features across 14 logical groups, built a 7-symbol behavioral vocabulary, and trained a 9-state Categorical HMM (selected by BIC from 70 fits across {2,3,4,5,7,9,11} state counts). Combined HMM posteriors with an inspector-presence proxy, gaming-suspicion score, and rule confirmation into a composite anomaly score, then segmented the suspicious population into 6 operationally distinct enforcement archetypes via K-means.
